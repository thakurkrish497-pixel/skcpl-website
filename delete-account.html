Account & Data Deletion Request

App: SKCPL — Connect to Waves | Developer: S. Kumar Creation Pvt. Ltd.

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies, users of SKCPL (Connect to Waves) have the right to request the deletion of their user account and associated personal data. Below are the steps to submit your deletion request and the breakdown of how your data is handled.

1 How to Request Account & Data Deletion

You can request account deletion using either of the following two methods:

mail Option A: Direct Email Request (Recommended)

Send an email to our data privacy team with your registered details:

  • To: info@skumarcreation.com
  • CC: krishthakur497@gmail.com
  • Subject: Account Deletion Request - SKCPL
  • Include: Your Full Name, Registered Mobile Number, and Site / Office Location.
send Click to Open Pre-Filled Email

smartphone Option B: Request via Admin / In-App

If you are currently logged into the app:

  1. Notify your site manager or company Administrator.
  2. Admin opens Manage Staff in the SKCPL app.
  3. Admin taps your profile and selects Deactivate / Delete Staff Account.
  4. Your account is immediately revoked from all devices.

2 Types of Data That Are Deleted

Upon processing your deletion request, the following information is permanently removed from our active databases:

  • check_circle User Account & Credentials: Your username, mobile number, password hashes, and active authentication session tokens.
  • check_circle Personal Profile Data: Profile photo, contact email, and employee personal identifiers.
  • check_circle GPS Location History: Historical live tracking breadcrumbs and temporary coordinates stored in cache.
  • check_circle Temporary Audio / Voice Notes: Raw audio recorded for speech-to-text processing.

3 Data That May Be Retained (Legal & Regulatory Compliance)

Certain business records created during your employment or site assignments cannot be immediately deleted due to statutory requirements under Indian law:

  • info Financial & Petty Cash Ledgers: Expense vouchers, challans, and bills logged into the cashbook are retained for up to 7 years in compliance with the Companies Act, 2013 and GST statutory audit regulations.
  • info Daily Work Reports (DSR) & Material Receipts: Construction site handover and quality compliance records are retained as required by client and government project contracts.

4 Processing Timeline

Account Access Revocation: Immediate (within 24 hours).

Database Record Deletion: Completed within 15 to 30 business days of identity verification.
